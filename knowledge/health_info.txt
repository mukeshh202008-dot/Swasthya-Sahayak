HEADACHE

A headache is pain or discomfort in the head or upper neck.
Common causes may include dehydration, lack of sleep, stress,
or other health conditions.

General self-care may include resting, drinking enough water,
and avoiding activities that make symptoms worse.

Seek urgent medical attention if a headache is sudden and
extremely severe, follows a serious injury, or occurs with
warning signs such as confusion, weakness, or difficulty speaking.

This information is for general awareness and is not a diagnosis.

HYDRATION

Adequate hydration supports brain function, digestion, circulation,
and overall energy levels. Water needs vary with activity, climate,
and health conditions.

Signs of dehydration may include thirst, dry mouth, dizziness,
fatigue, dark yellow urine, or reduced urination.

General self-care includes drinking water regularly throughout the day,
especially during exercise, hot weather, or illness with fever or diarrhea.

Seek medical care if dehydration is severe, if you are vomiting repeatedly,
or if there is confusion, fainting, or little urine output.

SLEEP

Sleep is essential for repair, immunity, memory, and emotional wellbeing.
Poor sleep can contribute to fatigue, irritability, poor concentration,
and a higher risk of illness.

Healthy sleep habits include keeping a regular sleep schedule,
avoiding bright screens before bed, limiting caffeine late in the day,
and creating a calm sleeping environment.

Seek medical advice if sleep problems continue for weeks,
if you snore heavily, gasp during sleep, or feel extremely tired during the day.

NUTRITION

A balanced diet includes fruits, vegetables, whole grains,
lean proteins, healthy fats, and adequate water.

Good nutrition supports immunity, digestion, energy, and long-term health.
Reducing excess sugar, highly processed foods, and large portions can help
improve overall wellbeing.

Seek medical advice if you have unexplained weight loss,
ongoing vomiting, severe abdominal pain, or persistent digestive issues.

FEVER

A fever is a temporary rise in body temperature often caused by infection.
Common symptoms include warmth, sweating, chills, weakness, and body aches.

General self-care includes rest, fluids, and a comfortable room temperature.
If the fever is mild and the person is otherwise stable, home care may be enough.

Seek urgent medical help if a fever is very high, lasts more than a few days,
comes with breathing problems, confusion, severe weakness, or a stiff neck.

COUGH

A cough is a common symptom that may be caused by colds, allergies,
air pollution, asthma, or infections.

General self-care includes rest, fluids, and avoiding smoke or irritants.
If the cough is related to an allergy, avoiding triggers may help.

Seek medical advice if the cough lasts more than 2 to 3 weeks,
causes chest pain, is associated with blood, or comes with weight loss,
shortness of breath, or fever.

MUSCLE OR BODY PAIN

Body aches can occur from exercise, poor sleep, dehydration, stress,
infection, or illness.

General self-care may include rest, hydration, gentle stretching,
heat or cold packs, and avoiding overexertion.

Seek medical help if pain is severe, sudden, persists for many days,
or comes with fever, swelling, redness, weakness, or trouble moving.

GENERAL SAFETY

Seek urgent medical care for chest pain, trouble breathing,
severe bleeding, fainting, confusion, a seizure, or sudden weakness.

Emergency help is also needed for severe dehydration, severe abdominal pain,
or symptoms that worsen rapidly.

For ongoing problems or uncertain symptoms, a healthcare professional can
assess, diagnose, and guide the next steps.

Always seek professional medical care if there are serious or worsening symptoms.
This information is for general health awareness only and is not a diagnosis.
